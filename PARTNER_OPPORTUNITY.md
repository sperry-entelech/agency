# Partner Opportunity — Entelech

## What Entelech Is

Entelech is a RevOps and AI consulting practice. We help B2B companies fix revenue leaks — slow lead follow-up, broken proposal processes, client churn in the first 90 days — by building bespoke AI agents on their operations.

We don't chain together one-off automations. We build agents — systems that learn your operations, handle multiple workflows holistically, and get better every week through human feedback. Most "AI consultants" build workflows — they define what happens AND how. We build agents — we define the goal, and the system figures out the how.

We're not a SaaS company. We're not an "AI agency." We're a consulting practice that builds things. The things we build are AI agents tailored to each client's tribal knowledge, edge cases, and specific software stack. The value is in the business outcomes: more leads converted, faster sales cycles, lower churn.

## The Thesis

Generic AI SaaS can't solve real business problems. Every business has unique tribal knowledge, edge cases, and software stacks that no off-the-shelf tool can account for. The companies that win will have bespoke agents built on THEIR operations — not someone else's template.

Mid-market businesses ($1M-$20M) can't hire internal teams to build this. They don't have AI engineers on staff. They don't have the time to evaluate twelve tools and stitch them together. But they need agents that understand their dispatch process, their proposal workflow, their client onboarding — the way their business actually runs, not the way a SaaS company imagines it runs.

That's the gap. First movers who systematize how to build and deliver bespoke agents own the market.

The agent advantage compounds. A good agent gets better every week. In 4 weeks, what started at 70% accuracy hits 99%+ through human feedback and error correction. That's not a tool — it's an employee that never stops improving.

The playbook is vertical-agnostic — what works for a transportation company works for a professional services firm works for a field service business. The agent frameworks translate. This isn't about being the smartest AI people in the room. It's about being the first to systematize how bespoke agents get built, delivered, and improved.

## What Makes This Different

Every week another person announces they're an "AI consultant." Most of them are selling ChatGPT tutorials or chaining together Zapier steps. Here's what's actually different about this:

1. **We don't build one-off automations.** We map your entire operation and build agents that handle multiple workflows holistically. Not one fix at a time — a system that understands how your business actually runs.
2. **Your agent is built on YOUR operations.** Not a template. Not a generic SaaS. Built on your tribal knowledge, your edge cases, your specific software stack. That's why it works where off-the-shelf tools fail.
3. **Agents that learn.** The system gets better every week through human feedback loops and error correction. 70% accuracy on day one → 99%+ in four weeks. That's not a static tool — it's a system that improves.
4. **Governance from day one.** Human-in-the-loop, audit trails, error correction built in from the start. The best agents are built by people who don't trust them — that's what makes them trustworthy.
5. **You own everything.** The agent runs on your infrastructure, integrates with your stack, works independently of us. No vendor lock-in. If you fire us tomorrow, your agent keeps running.

We're not competing on being the smartest. We're competing on building agents that actually work in the real world — because they're built on real operations, not abstractions.
