# Partnership Framework — Entelech

This document outlines the structure for partners joining Entelech. It's a framework, not a rigid contract — specific terms are discussed individually based on contribution type and level.

---

## Partner Types

### Operational Partner
You work in the business. Could be sales/outbound, operations, technical delivery, or a combination. Time commitment: 10-20 hours/week depending on role and arrangement.

### Capital Partner
You fund operating costs (AI tools, hosting, automation platforms, infrastructure). Lower time commitment, equity weighted toward financial contribution.

### Hybrid Partner
You contribute both time and capital. Most common arrangement — absorb some costs and work in a specific role.

---

## Equity & Revenue Share

### Founding Equity
Ethan retains majority equity as founder, technical lead, and primary builder of existing infrastructure. Partner equity is allocated based on:

- **Contribution type** — operational vs. capital vs. hybrid
- **Contribution level** — hours/week, dollar amount of costs absorbed, scope of role
- **Risk absorbed** — partners joining earlier (pre-revenue) take more risk, earn more equity

Equity ranges are discussed individually. There's no one-size-fits-all number. A sales partner working 15 hours/week and covering $500/month in tool costs earns differently than someone contributing capital only.

### Revenue Share
Once revenue starts flowing, partners who close or manage deals may earn revenue share on top of equity. Structure options include:

- **Percentage of revenue from deals you close** (typical range: 10-25%)
- **Commission per engagement** (flat fee per closed deal)
- **Performance tiers** (higher volume = higher percentage)

Revenue share model is chosen based on what makes sense for the business stage and the partner's role. This gets formalized after the trial period.

### Vesting
Equity vests over time to protect both sides.

- **90-day trial period:** No equity vests during trial. This is evaluation for both parties.
- **Post-trial vesting:** If trial succeeds, equity vests monthly over the following 9-12 months.
- **Full vesting:** Complete after 12 months total (3 months trial + 9 months vesting).

If a partner exits before full vesting, they retain only the equity vested to that point.

## Cost-Sharing

Partners absorbing operating costs contribute to some combination of:

- **AI tools** — Claude API credits, other AI service subscriptions
- **Automation platforms** — n8n, Make.com, or similar workflow tools
- **Hosting & infrastructure** — Vercel, Supabase, domain registrations
- **Email infrastructure** — Sending platforms, warm-up tools, email accounts
- **Sales tools** — CRM, prospecting tools, LinkedIn premium
- **Communication** — Video conferencing, collaboration platforms

Specific dollar amounts aren't pinned here because costs change and arrangements vary. The framework is: cost absorption maps to equity. More costs absorbed = more equity, within the ranges discussed for your partner type.

Monthly cost contributions are tracked transparently. Both parties have visibility into what's being spent and where.

## 90-Day Trial Period

Every partnership starts with a 90-day trial. This protects everyone.

### Purpose
- Evaluate working relationship and communication fit
- Test whether the role and time commitment work in practice
- Build enough data to make an informed decision about continuing
- No equity vests during this period — clean break if it doesn't work

### What Gets Measured
We use **input metrics, not output metrics.** 90 days isn't enough to judge revenue results reliably. Instead, we measure effort, consistency, and learning:

**For operational partners:**
- Consistent activity against agreed targets (proposals sent, calls booked, tasks completed)
- Weekly documentation of what's working and what isn't
- Attendance at regular check-ins
- Evidence of iteration and improvement

**For capital partners:**
- Consistent, on-time cost contributions
- Engagement with business updates and decisions
- Participation in monthly strategic reviews

### Trial Outcomes

**Continue (partnership working):**
- Hit 75%+ of agreed input targets
- Good communication and working relationship
- Equity vesting begins in month 4

**Extend 30 days (close but not there):**
- Hit 60-75% of targets, or faced legitimate obstacles
- Modified targets for extended trial
- Decision at day 120

**Restructure (different role might work):**
- Current role isn't the right fit
- Explore advisory, referral, or capital-only arrangement
- Adjusted equity to match new contribution level

**Clean break (not a fit):**
- No hard feelings, no equity retained
- Both parties keep learnings
- Ethan retains full business ownership

## Performance Expectations by Role

### Sales / Outbound Partner
- **Monthly targets:** Agreed number of outreach activities, discovery calls, proposals
- **Focus:** Client acquisition using existing systems and templates
- **Owns:** Sales process, pipeline management, client qualification
- **Reports:** Weekly activity + learnings, monthly pipeline review

### Operations Partner
- **Monthly targets:** Project milestones delivered, client communication maintained, processes documented
- **Focus:** Delivery coordination and client experience
- **Owns:** Project timelines, client communication, operational processes
- **Reports:** Weekly project status, monthly delivery review

### Technical Delivery Partner
- **Monthly targets:** Systems built/deployed, technical quality standards met
- **Focus:** Building client automation and AI systems
- **Owns:** Technical implementation within assigned projects
- **Reports:** Weekly build progress, monthly technical review

### Capital Partner
- **Monthly targets:** Cost contributions delivered on time
- **Focus:** Financial support for operating infrastructure
- **Owns:** Agreed portion of operating costs
- **Reports:** Monthly financial review, quarterly strategic review

## Decision Rights

### Ethan retains final say on:
- Technical architecture and tool selection
- Delivery timelines and quality standards
- Service offering scope (what we can and can't deliver)
- Client onboarding and implementation process
- Infrastructure and security decisions
- Overall business strategy and positioning

### Partners own their domain:
- Sales partners have autonomy over sales messaging, channel selection, and outreach cadence (within brand framework)
- Operations partners have autonomy over project management approach and client communication style
- Technical partners have autonomy over implementation approach within defined standards

### Joint decisions required for:
- Major pricing changes
- New service offerings or market pivots
- Bringing on additional partners
- Major expenses outside normal operations
- Brand positioning changes

### Conflict resolution:
1. Direct discussion to understand perspectives
2. Data-driven decision (test both approaches if possible)
3. Ethan's call on strategic/technical matters, partner's call on their domain
4. External advisor if genuinely stuck

## Exit Scenarios

### Partner exits during trial (days 1-90)
- Zero equity retained
- Partner keeps learnings; Ethan retains business IP
- Any client relationships transitioned to Ethan
- Clean break, no ongoing obligations

### Partner exits during vesting (months 4-12)
- Partner retains equity vested to that point
- Ethan may offer buyout of vested equity to simplify cap table
- 30-60 day transition period for any client relationships
- Non-compete: none (we don't do non-competes for early partners)

### Partner exits after full vesting (month 12+)
- Partner retains full vested equity
- Buyout negotiable at fair valuation if desired
- Comprehensive transition plan for responsibilities
- Can remain as passive equity holder or exit via buyout

### Business pivot or major change
- Both parties discuss and agree on direction
- If a partner disagrees with a pivot, clean exit with vested equity retained
- Business continuity prioritized over any individual arrangement

## Legal & Administrative

### Business Structure
- LLC formation planned (provides liability protection)
- Formal operating agreement supplements this framework
- All client contracts in business name, not personal names

### Intellectual Property
- Pre-existing IP (everything Ethan built before partnership) remains Ethan's
- New IP created during partnership owned proportionally per equity split
- Exiting partner's IP share available for buyout

### Confidentiality
Both parties maintain confidentiality of:
- Client information and data
- Business financials
- Partnership terms and discussions
- Proprietary systems and methodologies
