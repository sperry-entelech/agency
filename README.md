# Entelech — RevOps & AI Consulting

Revenue operations consulting for B2B businesses. We build bespoke AI agents on your operations — systems that learn your business, handle multiple workflows, and get better every week.

---

## What We Do

Entelech helps businesses close revenue gaps — the money they're losing to slow lead follow-up, broken proposal processes, and early client churn. We build bespoke AI agents tailored to your operations. Not templates. Not generic SaaS. Systems built on your tribal knowledge, your edge cases, your specific software stack — that get better every week through human feedback.

**Core Services:**
- **Cold Outreach Infrastructure** — Client-owned sending setup ($500-$1,500). The entry point: we build the outbound machine, you own it. Optional managed outreach at $500/month.
- **Lead Conversion Agent** — Scores, routes, and follows up with leads automatically. Learns which leads convert and improves over time.
- **Sales Velocity Agent** — AI-assisted proposal generation, adaptive follow-up sequences, pipeline optimization. Faster cycles, higher close rates.
- **Client Retention Agent** — Onboarding coordination, proactive engagement, churn early warning. The agent learns what keeps clients.
- **Full RevOps Agent** — Comprehensive agent across the entire customer lifecycle. One system, multiple workflows, holistic intelligence.

**Business Model:** Outbound infrastructure is fixed-price, client-owned. Agent engagements are value-based, risk-free — clients pay only when they see measurable results. No IP restrictions across any tier.

## Service Delivery

Every engagement follows our 6-phase framework:

1. **Discovery** — Understand the business, identify problems, define success
2. **Architecture** — Design the solution, client approves before we build
3. **Development** — Build with AI-assisted development (3-5x faster)
4. **Deployment** — Staged rollout, tested in production conditions
5. **Quality Assurance** — Validate against real-world conditions
6. **Documentation & Handoff** — Client owns and operates independently
