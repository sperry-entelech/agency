## SERVICE OFFERINGS

### Entry Point: Cold Outreach Infrastructure
Client-owned outbound sending infrastructure — domains, inboxes, sequences, all in the client's accounts. Three tiers: Infrastructure Setup ($500), Launch Package ($1,500), Managed Outreach ($500/month or per-meeting). The entry point that leads to full agent engagements.

### 1. Lead Conversion Agent
Bespoke agent that handles lead scoring, routing, qualification, and follow-up across your entire lead pipeline. Learns which leads convert and improves scoring over time. Response time drops from hours to minutes.

### 2. Sales Velocity Agent
AI-powered proposal generation, adaptive follow-up sequences, pipeline optimization. The agent learns your winning proposal patterns and accelerates your sales cycle.

### 3. Client Retention Agent
Onboarding coordination, proactive engagement, early warning systems for churn risk. Compresses onboarding from weeks to days. The agent learns what keeps clients and flags risks early.

### 4. Full RevOps Agent
Comprehensive agent across the entire customer lifecycle — lead conversion + sales velocity + client retention + reporting. One system, multiple workflows, holistic intelligence that sees how each function connects.

## BRAND VOICE & POSITIONING

### Voice Characteristics
- **Authentic & Direct** — No corporate speak, honest about where we are
- **Data-Driven & Specific** — Concrete details, not vague promises
- **Results-Focused** — Everything ties to measurable business outcomes
- **Human** — Conversational tone, not stuffy business language
- **Contrarian When Earned** — Challenge norms (like upfront payment) only when we can back it up

### Anti-AI Slop Standards
All content must score 8+ on our 10-point scale:
1. **Specificity (2 pts):** Concrete numbers, names, timeframes, dollar amounts
2. **Voice Authenticity (2 pts):** Conversational, opinionated, real
3. **Hook Strength (2 pts):** Immediate value or surprise — no generic opens
4. **Value Density (2 pts):** Every sentence delivers insight, no filler
5. **AI Red Flags (2 pts):** No "let's dive in," "in conclusion," emoji overuse, generic CTAs
